## 服务守卫统一检查式

以下是用例服务的可执行谓词规格，不声称已实现HTTP或加密设施。

```text
quotaPublish := count(PUBLISH,user, t in (now-24h,now]) < 10
quotaClaim   := count(CLAIM,user,found, t in (now-24h,now]) < 3
caseDeadline := min(initialWaiting+7d,caseCreated+180d,evidenceDeadline)
newDeadline  := min(oldDeadline,caseDeadline)    # 只缩短
evidencePurge:= min(endedAt+90d,foundPurge,lostPurge,deactivationTime)
canConfirm  := SELECTED and now<deadline and pairVersionsEqual
               and noFlagsOnBoth and placeEnabledInSameCampus
canReadMedia:= currentSession and currentScopeAuthorization and now<contentPurge
candidateStore:= score>=40 and lostVersionCurrent and foundVersionCurrent
                 and ruleEpochCurrent and fenceCurrent
```

服务错误不返回SQL堆栈。23505唯一冲突与23514状态不变量按动作映射409，用户字段先校验则400；数据库不可用503。涉及实际解码、真样本、当前时间/权限、NT-1自由文本禁止和密钥设施的守卫均须在未来集成/端到端验证，不能仅据DDL认定通过。
